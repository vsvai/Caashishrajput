Ashish Jayalata & Associates handles the incorporation of Private Limited Companies, One Person Companies (OPCs), and Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs) for entrepreneurs and businesses in Ghaziabad, Sahibabad, and across the NCR. The practice manages the entire process from name approval to certificate of incorporation, including DIN/DSC application, MOA/AOA preparation, and post-incorporation ROC filings.

What This Includes

  • Private Limited Company Incorporation: End-to-end incorporation of a Private Limited Company under the Companies Act 2013 using the SPICe+ integrated form, including name reservation, MOA and AOA preparation, DIN allotment, and certificate of incorporation from the Registrar of Companies (ROC).
  • One Person Company (OPC) Registration: Incorporation of an OPC for a single entrepreneur, with nomination of a nominee director, and compliance with the specific requirements applicable to OPCs under the Companies Act 2013.
  • LLP Incorporation: Registration of a Limited Liability Partnership under the LLP Act, 2008, including filing of Form FiLLiP (Form for incorporation of LLP), LLP agreement drafting, and allotment of LLP Identification Number (LLPIN).
  • DIN & DSC Application: Application for Director Identification Number (DIN) for proposed directors and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for authorized signatories, as prerequisites for company incorporation.
  • Name Approval: Application for name availability and reservation through the RUN (Reserve Unique Name) service or SPICe+ form, with advisory on name availability and compliance with naming guidelines under the Companies Act 2013.
  • ROC Post-Incorporation Filings: Filing of declarations (INC-20A for commencement of business), appointment of first directors, registered office verification, and other post-incorporation compliance filings.
  • Director Appointment & Changes: Filing of forms for appointment, resignation, or change of directors, including DIR-12 and related filings with ROC.
  • Registered Office Change: Filing of Form INC-22 for change of registered office within the same state or from one state to another, including preparation of supporting documents and board resolutions.

Who It's For

This service is relevant for entrepreneurs looking to start a business, existing business owners converting to a company or LLP structure, and individuals or groups seeking limited liability protection and formal business entity status. It applies across all sectors — trading, manufacturing, services, technology, and professional practices.

Our Process

1
Consultation — Understanding the business objectives and advising on the appropriate entity type (Private Limited Company, OPC, or LLP) based on the number of founders, capital requirements, and long-term plans.
2
Name Approval & DSC/DIN — Applying for name reservation with ROC, obtaining Digital Signature Certificates for proposed directors, and applying for DIN.
3
Document Preparation & Filing — Drafting MOA/AOA (for companies) or LLP agreement, preparing all supporting documents, and filing the incorporation application with MCA.
4
Incorporation & Post-Incorporation Compliance — Obtaining the certificate of incorporation, and completing post-incorporation filings — INC-20A, director appointments, registered office verification, PAN/TAN applications, and bank account opening support.

Documents Required

  • PAN Card of all proposed directors/partners
  • Aadhaar Card of all proposed directors/partners
  • Address proof of all proposed directors/partners (utility bill, bank statement)
  • Passport-size photographs of directors/partners
  • Registered office address proof (rental agreement and NOC from owner, or property ownership document)
  • MOA and AOA (for company incorporation — drafted by the practice)
  • LLP agreement (for LLP incorporation — drafted by the practice)
  • Nomination form (for OPC — nominee details)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a private limited company and an LLP?
A private limited company has shareholders, directors, and compliance under the Companies Act; an LLP has partners and is governed by the LLP Act. Both give limited liability, but an LLP has lighter compliance and no requirement for a minimum paid-up capital. We help you choose the structure that fits your business, tax, and growth plans.
How long does company registration take in Ghaziabad?
Once documents and the digital signature certificates (DSC) are ready, a private limited company can typically be incorporated within 7-10 working days, depending on MCA processing. LLP registration is usually faster. We handle name approval, SPICe+ filing, PAN and TAN application, and the certificate of incorporation end to end.
How much does it cost to register a company?
The cost includes government fees and stamp duty, which depend on the state and authorised capital, plus professional fees for preparing documents, digital signatures, and filing. We give you a complete quote covering both government and professional charges before we begin, so there are no surprises later.
What documents are needed for company registration?
You need PAN and address proof of the proposed directors, passport-size photographs, proof of the registered office address such as an electricity bill and rent agreement, and Aadhaar for DSC issuance. For a foreign director, passport and other documents are required. We prepare the incorporation forms from these.
Can one person incorporate a company in India?
Yes. A one-person company (OPC) allows a single person to incorporate a company with limited liability. Only an individual who is an Indian citizen and resident is eligible. If your business is likely to have multiple promoters soon, a private limited company with two directors may be a better fit.
What compliance is required after incorporation?
A company must file annual returns, hold board meetings, and maintain statutory registers; an LLP files annual Form 8 and Form 11 returns. Compliances start from the first year. We offer a company secretarial and compliance plan that keeps your filings done on time and avoids penalties.
What are the government fees for company registration?
Government fees depend on the state of registration, the type of entity, and authorised capital. Stamp duty also varies by state. For example, the fee for a private limited company is based on authorised capital slabs set by the MCA. We calculate the exact government charges applicable to your case and include them in your quote.
