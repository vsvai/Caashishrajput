Company & LLP Registration

Incorporation and compliance for Private Limited Companies, OPCs, and LLPs

Ashish Jayalata & Associates handles the incorporation of Private Limited Companies, One Person Companies (OPCs), and Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs) for entrepreneurs and businesses in Ghaziabad, Sahibabad, and across the NCR. The practice manages the entire process from name approval to certificate of incorporation, including DIN/DSC application, MOA/AOA preparation, and post-incorporation ROC filings.

What This Includes

  • Private Limited Company Incorporation: End-to-end incorporation of a Private Limited Company under the Companies Act 2013 using the SPICe+ integrated form, including name reservation, MOA and AOA preparation, DIN allotment, and certificate of incorporation from the Registrar of Companies (ROC).
  • One Person Company (OPC) Registration: Incorporation of an OPC for a single entrepreneur, with nomination of a nominee director, and compliance with the specific requirements applicable to OPCs under the Companies Act 2013.
  • LLP Incorporation: Registration of a Limited Liability Partnership under the LLP Act, 2008, including filing of Form FiLLiP (Form for incorporation of LLP), LLP agreement drafting, and allotment of LLP Identification Number (LLPIN).
  • DIN & DSC Application: Application for Director Identification Number (DIN) for proposed directors and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for authorized signatories, as prerequisites for company incorporation.
  • Name Approval: Application for name availability and reservation through the RUN (Reserve Unique Name) service or SPICe+ form, with advisory on name availability and compliance with naming guidelines under the Companies Act 2013.
  • ROC Post-Incorporation Filings: Filing of declarations (INC-20A for commencement of business), appointment of first directors, registered office verification, and other post-incorporation compliance filings.
  • Director Appointment & Changes: Filing of forms for appointment, resignation, or change of directors, including DIR-12 and related filings with ROC.
  • Registered Office Change: Filing of Form INC-22 for change of registered office within the same state or from one state to another, including preparation of supporting documents and board resolutions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a Private Limited Company and an LLP?
A Private Limited Company has separate legal entity status, limited liability for shareholders, and can raise equity capital. It requires minimum 2 directors and 2 shareholders, and must comply with ROC filings, board meetings, and audit requirements. An LLP offers flexible management, no minimum capital requirement, and simpler compliance. Partners have limited liability, but LLPs cannot raise equity capital and have restrictions on certain business activities.
How long does company or LLP registration take?
Company registration typically takes 7 to 15 working days, depending on MCA processing times and documentation completeness. LLP registration may take 10 to 20 working days. The timeline depends on name approval, DIN/DSC processing, and any observations from the Registrar of Companies.
What is a Director Identification Number (DIN)?
DIN (Director Identification Number) is a unique identification number assigned to every individual appointed as a director of a company. It is applied for through the SPICe+ form (for new companies) or DIR-3 form (for existing companies). DIN is mandatory under Section 153 of the Companies Act 2013 and is valid for the lifetime of the director.
What are the annual compliance requirements for a company?
Annual compliance requirements include holding at least 4 board meetings per year, filing annual return (MGT-7) and financial statements (AOC-4) with ROC, conducting statutory audit, holding annual general meeting (AGM), filing income tax returns, and maintaining statutory registers. LLPs must file annual return (Form 11) and statement of accounts (Form 8).
