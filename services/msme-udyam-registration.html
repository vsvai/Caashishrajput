Registration and advisory for micro, small, and medium enterprises

Ashish Jayalata & Associates assists businesses in Ghaziabad, Sahibabad, and across the NCR with Udyam registration (formerly Udyog Aadhaar) for classification as a micro, small, or medium enterprise under the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act, 2006. The practice handles new registration, revision, amendment, and advisory on the benefits available to registered MSMEs.

What This Includes

  • New Udyam Registration: Online application on the Udyam Registration Portal (udyamregistration.gov.in) for classification of the business as a micro, small, or medium enterprise, with generation of the Udyam Registration Number (URN) and certificate.
  • Registration Amendment: Updating existing Udyam registration details for changes in business activity, investment in plant and machinery or equipment, turnover, address, or ownership structure.
  • Classification Advisory: Advising on the applicable MSME classification based on investment and turnover thresholds, and guidance on maintaining the correct classification for eligibility to MSME benefits.
  • Benefits Advisory: Guidance on benefits available to registered MSMEs — priority sector lending, protection against delayed payments under Section 15 of the MSMED Act, government procurement preferences, and access to MSME schemes and subsidies.
  • Delayed Payment Protection: Advisory on the MSMED Act provisions for recovery of payments from buyers who fail to make payment within 45 days of goods or services delivery, including the right to compound interest on delayed payments.
  • Government Scheme Eligibility: Information on central and state government MSME schemes — Credit Guarantee Fund (CGTMSE), Technology Upgradation Fund Scheme (TUFS), MSME Samadhaan (delayed payment portal), and other relevant programs.
  • Integration with Other Registrations: Coordination with GST registration, PAN, and other business registrations to ensure consistency in registered details.

Who It's For

This service is relevant for manufacturers, traders, service providers, and any business entity that qualifies as a micro, small, or medium enterprise under the MSMED Act. It applies to sole proprietorships, partnerships, Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs), companies, LLPs, and any other entity engaged in production, manufacturing, or service delivery in Ghaziabad, Sahibabad, and across NCR.

Documents Required

  • PAN Card (of the business entity or individual)
  • Aadhaar Card (of the entrepreneur/authorized person)
  • Business address proof (utility bill, property document, or rental agreement)

  • Business registration certificate (if applicable — partnership deed, incorporation certificate, etc.)

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I register my business under Udyam?
Udyam registration classifies your business as micro, small, or medium and gives access to benefits under the MSMED Act — priority lending, lower interest rates on some schemes, eligibility for government tenders, and protection against delayed payments. It is a simple online registration and there is no government fee for it.
How much does MSME Udyam registration cost?
The Udyam registration itself is free on the official portal. If you use a professional service to prepare and submit the application, you pay only a modest service fee. We keep our fee for Udyam registration low because the process is straightforward — no hidden or government charges are passed on to you.
How long does Udyam registration take in Ghaziabad?
Udyam registration is a quick online process. Once we have your PAN, Aadhaar, and business details, the application can be submitted the same day, and the Udyam certificate is usually issued immediately. If a correction or reclassification is needed, we handle the rectification on the portal.
Who is eligible for Udyam registration?
Any business — proprietorship, partnership, company, or LLP — engaged in manufacturing or providing services is eligible, based on investment in plant and machinery or equipment and annual turnover. The definitions of micro, small, and medium are set out in the MSMED Act based on investment and turnover.
What documents are needed for Udyam registration?
Udyam registration is based on self-declaration. You need your PAN and Aadhaar, the business name and address, and details of investment in plant and machinery or equipment and annual turnover. For most cases, no supporting documents need to be uploaded. We confirm the classification before submission.
What government schemes can an Udyam-registered business access?
Registered MSMEs can access schemes such as priority sector lending, collateral-free loans under CGTMSE, subsidies under schemes like PMEGP, and interest subvention on some loans. Udyam registration also improves your eligibility for government procurement tenders and delayed payment protection.
Do I need to update my Udyam registration details?
Yes. The Udyam portal allows you to update business details and reclassify as your investment or turnover changes. Updating your registration keeps it accurate and protects your eligibility for MSME benefits. We can help you make updates and check that your classification reflects your current turnover.
