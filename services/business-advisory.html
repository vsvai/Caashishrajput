Ashish Jayalata and Associates provides business advisory services to companies, LLPs, and businesses in Ghaziabad, Sahibabad, and across the NCR. The practice offers virtual CFO support, budgeting and cash flow management, capital structure advisory, fund-raising guidance, NRI taxation, FEMA compliance, and IFRS and Ind AS advisory, helping businesses make informed financial decisions at various stages of growth.

What This Includes

  • Virtual CFO: Part-time or outsourced CFO services for businesses that do not require a full-time in-house CFO. This includes financial planning, budgeting, cash flow management, financial reporting, and strategic financial input on a monthly retainer basis.
  • Budgeting and Forecasting: Preparation of annual budgets, variance analysis, and rolling forecasts to support business planning and resource allocation.
  • Cash Flow Management: Analysis and forecasting of cash flows, identification of cash flow gaps, and recommendations for working capital optimization.
  • Capital Structure Advisory: Advisory on the appropriate mix of debt and equity financing based on the business risk profile, growth plans, and cost of capital considerations.
  • Fund-Raising Advisory: Guidance on raising capital through equity, debt, or hybrid instruments, including preparation of financial projections and information memoranda for potential investors or lenders.
  • NRI Taxation: Advisory and computation support for Non-Resident Indians NRIs on residential status determination, tax liability, double taxation avoidance agreement DTAA benefits, and repatriation of income and assets.
  • FEMA Compliance: Advisory on compliance with the Foreign Exchange Management Act FEMA, including reporting of FDI, ECB, ODI, and other cross-border transactions, as well as compounding applications for FEMA violations.
  • IFRS and Ind AS Advisory: Advisory on the adoption and implementation of Indian Accounting Standards Ind AS and International Financial Reporting Standards IFRS, including first-time adoption, transition support, and ongoing compliance.

Who It Is For

This service is relevant for startups, small and medium enterprises, growing companies, and established businesses in Ghaziabad, Sahibabad, and the NCR that require strategic financial input without the overhead of a full-time CFO, or that need specialized advisory on fund-raising, FEMA, or accounting standards conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a virtual CFO service?
A virtual CFO is an outsourced senior finance professional who works part-time with your business — budgeting, cash flow planning, management reporting, and financial decision support, without the cost of a full-time CFO. We act as your finance partner while your in-house team handles the day-to-day accounting.
Is virtual CFO service suitable for a small business in Ghaziabad?
Yes. Virtual CFO support is particularly useful for growing businesses that need financial structure but cannot justify a full-time CFO. Whether you are raising funds, planning to expand, or trying to improve margins, a part-time finance function supports budgeting, cash flow and reporting without a full-time hire.
What will I get from a business advisory engagement?
You get a budget and cash flow forecast, monthly management accounts with commentary, KPI tracking, cost and margin analysis, and strategic advice on pricing, funding, and expansion. We also review your finance processes and recommend improvements, and we support you during fund-raising or loan applications.
How much does a virtual CFO cost?
Virtual CFO fees depend on the size of your business and the depth of support you need — from a few hours a month for light advisory to a structured retainer with monthly deliverables. We design a scope that fits your budget, and you can scale it up as your business grows.
Can you prepare the documents for a business loan or investor funding?
Yes. We prepare the financial projections, business plan, and the financial data banks ask for, and we review your loan application package for gaps. We do not arrange loans or act for any lender. For equity or investor funding, we help you present your financials clearly and answer investor due diligence questions.
Is advisory the same as my regular accounting?
No. Accounting records the past — maintaining books and preparing statements. Advisory focuses on the future — planning, budgeting, forecasting, and decisions. Many clients start with our accounting service and add advisory support as they grow, and we can combine both into a single engagement.
Will you provide management dashboards?
We provide simple management dashboards covering sales, profitability, cash position, and key ratios, updated as part of the monthly reporting cycle. The dashboards are designed for business owners who want to see the health of the business at a glance, not for accountants.
