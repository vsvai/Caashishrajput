Frequently Asked Questions

Who is required to file an Income Tax Return?
Any individual whose total income during the financial year exceeds the basic exemption limit (currently ₹2.5 lakh for individuals below 60 years) must file an ITR. Additionally, persons carrying on business or profession, those claiming a refund, persons with foreign assets or income, and those required to file under specific sections of the Act must file irrespective of income level.
What is the due date for filing income tax returns?
For individuals and entities not requiring an audit, the due date is generally 31st July of the assessment year. For persons whose accounts are subject to audit under Section 44AB, the due date is 31st October. For transfer pricing cases, the due date is 30th November.
Can I file my income tax return after the due date?
Yes, a belated return can be filed under Section 139(4) before the end of the relevant assessment year (generally by 31st December). However, a late filing fee under Section 234F applies — ₹5,000 if filed by 31st December, or ₹1,000 in other cases, subject to certain conditions. Interest under Section 234A may also apply if tax is due.
What is advance tax and who needs to pay it?
Advance tax is income tax paid in installments during the financial year, as per Section 208 of the Income Tax Act. It is applicable when the estimated total tax liability exceeds ₹10,000. For non-salaried taxpayers, installments are due on 15th June, 15th September, 15th December, and 15th March.
How does the faceless assessment scheme work?
Under the National Faceless Assessment Scheme, assessment proceedings are conducted electronically without any physical interface between the taxpayer and the assessing officer. The case is assigned to an assessment unit in a different location, and all communications happen through the e-proceedings facility.
