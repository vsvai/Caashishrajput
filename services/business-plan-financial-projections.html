Structured planning and financial modelling for startups and growing businesses

Ashish Jayalata and Associates prepares business plans and financial projections for startups, small and medium enterprises, and growing businesses in Ghaziabad, Sahibabad, and across the NCR. The practice develops financial models with revenue and expense projections, cash flow forecasts, break-even analysis, and supporting documentation for bank loan applications, investor presentations, and internal business planning.

What This Includes

  • Business Plan Preparation: Comprehensive business plan document covering executive summary, business description, market analysis, products or services, marketing strategy, organizational structure, operational plan, and financial summary.
  • Financial Modelling: Development of detailed financial models with revenue drivers, cost structure, capital expenditure projections, working capital requirements, and debt service schedules.
  • Revenue and Expense Projections: Projection of revenue streams, cost of goods sold, operating expenses, and other income and expenditure over a 3 to 5 year horizon, with monthly or quarterly detail as required.
  • Cash Flow Forecasting: Preparation of projected cash flow statements showing inflows and outflows, net cash position, and funding requirements over the projection period.
  • Break-Even Analysis: Computation of the break-even point -- the level of revenue at which total costs equal total income -- with sensitivity analysis on key assumptions such as pricing, volume, and cost changes.
  • Loan Application Documentation: Preparation of project reports, financial projections, and supporting documents required by banks and financial institutions for term loan, working capital, or project finance applications.
  • Budget Preparation: Annual and quarterly budgets with variance analysis templates, to support ongoing financial planning and performance monitoring.

Who It Is For

This service is relevant for entrepreneurs starting a new business, existing businesses seeking bank financing, companies preparing for investor engagement, and businesses requiring structured financial planning support. It applies to entities of all types -- proprietorships, partnerships, companies, and LLPs -- operating in Ghaziabad, Sahibabad, and the NCR region.

Our Process

1
Consultation -- Understanding the business concept, target market, revenue model, cost structure, and specific requirements for the business plan or projections.
2
Data Collection -- Gathering market research data, cost estimates, existing financial data (if any), and any other information required for building the financial model.
3
Model Building -- Developing the financial model with assumptions, projecting revenue and expenses, computing cash flows, and performing break-even and sensitivity analysis.
4
Draft Preparation and Finalization -- Preparing the business plan document or project report with financial tables, charts, and narrative, followed by review and finalization with the client.

Documents Required

  • Business concept description and value proposition
  • Market research data or industry reports (if available)
  • Cost estimates for setup, operations, and capital expenditure
  • Existing financial data (previous year P and L, balance sheet, if any)
  • Details of proposed funding -- equity, debt, or mixed
  • Business registration documents (for existing businesses)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a business plan?
A business plan typically covers your business model, products or services, market analysis, operations, management team, and financial projections. It is used for bank loans, investor meetings, or as an internal roadmap. We structure it for the audience you are approaching — banks, investors, or your own management.
Do banks accept your business plans for loans?
Yes. We prepare business plans and financial projections in a format banks and financial institutions expect, including realistic profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash flow projections and repayment capacity analysis. A well-prepared plan significantly strengthens a loan application, especially for MSME and term loans.
What information do you need from me?
We need details of your business model, historical financials where available, the purpose of the funding, and your assumptions about sales, costs, and working capital. If you are a new business without history, we work from your market research and projections. We guide you through what to provide.
How long does it take to prepare a business plan?
A business plan with financial projections is typically delivered within one to two weeks, depending on the completeness of the information you share. If financial modelling or market research is required, it may take a little longer. We agree on a timeline before starting.
How much does a business plan cost in Ghaziabad?
The fee depends on the complexity of the business, the depth of the financial model, and whether market research is required. We quote a fixed fee after understanding your requirement — for a bank loan plan, an investor plan, or an internal plan. No hidden charges.
What are financial projections and how far ahead do you project?
Financial projections estimate your future revenue, expenses, profit, and cash flow, typically for three to five years. They are built on assumptions about growth, pricing, and costs, and are presented month-wise for the first year. Banks and investors rely on these to assess repayment and returns.
Can I use the same plan for investors and banks?
A bank plan focuses on repayment capacity and security; an investor plan focuses on growth, valuation, and exit. We adjust the emphasis, assumptions, and presentation for each audience. If you are raising funds and taking a loan together, we prepare one consistent set of projections for both.
