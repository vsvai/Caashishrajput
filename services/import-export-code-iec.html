Import Export Code (IEC)

Mandatory registration for businesses engaged in import or export of goods

Ashish Jayalata & Associates handles the application and amendment of Import Export Code (IEC) from the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) for businesses in Ghaziabad, Sahibabad, and across the NCR. IEC is a mandatory 10-digit registration number required for any person or entity engaged in the import or export of goods from India, as per the Foreign Trade Policy.

What This Includes

  • New IEC Application: Online application on the DGFT portal (dgft.gov.in) with preparation of all required documents, including PAN, Aadhaar, business registration proof, bank account details, and digital photograph.
  • IEC Amendment: Amendment of existing IEC for changes in business name, address, constitution (proprietorship to partnership, partnership to company, etc.), bank account details, or other registered particulars.
  • PAN Linking: Ensuring proper linkage of IEC with the business entity's PAN, as IEC is PAN-based and any changes in PAN require corresponding updates in IEC records.
  • IEC Reactivation: Reactivation of deactivated or surrendered IEC where the business has resumed import/export activities, or where IEC was deactivated due to non-updation.
  • FTP Compliance Advisory: Advisory on compliance with the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP), including classification of goods, import/export procedures, documentation requirements, and eligibility for benefits under the FTP.
  • Digital Signature & DGFT Portal Access: Assistance with obtaining the required Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for signing the IEC application on the DGFT portal, and setting up of DGFT portal access.
  • Annual Updating: Guidance on annual updating of IEC details on the DGFT portal as recommended for keeping the registration active and valid.

Who It's For

This service is applicable to any business or individual engaged in or planning to engage in import or export of goods from India. It covers sole proprietorships, partnership firms, companies, LLPs, and any other entity type. IEC is also required for services exporters where the service provider is not covered under the automatic route.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IEC mandatory for all imports?
Yes, IEC is mandatory for any person or entity importing or exporting goods or services from India. No person can undertake import or export without an IEC, except certain categories exempted under the Foreign Trade Policy (such as imports for personal use, or government departments).
How do I apply for IEC?
IEC application is made online through the DGFT portal (dgft.gov.in). The applicant needs a valid PAN, Aadhaar, business registration proof, bank account details, and a digital photograph. The application is processed by the regional DGFT authority, and the IEC is allotted upon successful verification — typically within 1 to 3 working days.
What is the validity of IEC?
IEC is valid for the lifetime of the entity and does not require periodic renewal. However, it must be updated if there are changes in business details such as name, address, constitution, or bank account. Annual updating on the DGFT portal is recommended to keep the registration active.
