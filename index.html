About Ashish Jayalata & Associates

CA Ashish Rajput is a Chartered Accountancy practice based in Shalimar Garden, Sahibabad, Ghaziabad. The practice provides accounting, audit, direct and indirect tax, and business compliance services to individuals, proprietorships, partnerships, and companies across Ghaziabad and the wider NCR region.

The practice is registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). Our work covers income tax filing and planning, GST registration and compliance, statutory and internal audit, company and LLP incorporation, and related advisory and compliance work.
