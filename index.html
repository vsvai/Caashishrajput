How we work

What to expect when you engage us

The same four steps apply whether the work is a single return or an annual audit.

  1. Tell us the requirement

    Call, message on WhatsApp, send an enquiry or visit the office. Share any notice or deadline you are working against.

  2. Scope and documents agreed

    We confirm the scope of work, the documents needed and the fee before any work starts.

  3. Work prepared for your review

    Returns, filings and reports are prepared and shared with you for review before they are submitted.

  4. Filing and follow-up

    We file, share acknowledgements and track the next statutory due dates that apply to you.
