Income Tax

End-to-end Income Tax compliance and advisory for individuals, Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs), proprietorships, partnerships, and companies.

What this includes

  • Individual ITR filing (salaried, business income, capital gains, foreign income)
  • Business/profession ITR filing for proprietorships, firms, and companies
  • Tax planning and advance tax computation
  • Response to Income Tax notices and assessments
  • Appeals before CIT(A) and ITAT
  • TDS/TCS compliance and return filing
  • Faceless assessment and penalty proceedings

Who it's for

Salaried individuals, self-employed professionals, business owners, partners in firms, directors of companies, and NRIs with Indian-source income.

GST

Complete Goods and Services Tax compliance, from registration to return filing and audit support.

What this includes

  • GST registration (new, amendment, cancellation)
  • Monthly/quarterly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filing
  • Annual return filing (GSTR-9, GSTR-9C)
  • GST reconciliation and input tax credit matching
  • GST audit and compliance review
  • Response to GST notices and show-cause proceedings
  • GST litigation support before Appellate Authority

Who it's for

Businesses registered or required to register under GST, including traders, manufacturers, service providers, e-commerce sellers, and composition scheme dealers.

Need help with GST compliance? Reach out to discuss your requirement.

Audit & Assurance

Statutory, internal, and tax audit services conducted in accordance with Standards on Auditing issued by ICAI.

What this includes

  • Statutory audit of companies and LLPs
  • Tax audit under Section 44AB of the Income Tax Act
  • Internal audit and risk-based audit for businesses
  • Compliance audit and special purpose audit
  • Bank audit (statutory and concurrent)
  • Audit of charitable trusts and NPOs

Who it's for

Companies (private and public), LLPs, partnership firms, trusts, societies, and entities required to undergo mandatory tax or statutory audit.

Company & LLP Registration

Business incorporation, ongoing ROC compliance, and company law advisory.

What this includes

  • LLP (Limited Liability Partnership) incorporation

  • ROC annual filings and compliance (AOC-4, MGT-7)
  • Director appointment, resignation, and DIN allotment
  • Company name approval and objecion handling
  • Registered office change and authorized capital alteration
