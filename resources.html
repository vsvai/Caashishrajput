How to read this: monthly entries refer to returns/payments of the previous month unless otherwise stated. Statutory dates may change by notification — always confirm on the official portal (GST, income tax, EPFO/ESI, MCA) before filing.

Other GST returns: GSTR-5 (non-resident taxable persons, 13th) and GSTR-5A (OIDAR, 20th). Monthly vs quarterly GSTR-1/GSTR-3B depends on turnover and QRMP enrolment.

Event-based filings (no fixed calendar date): ADT-1 for the first auditor within 15 days of the board appointing them (board must appoint within 30 days of incorporation); MGT-14 within 30 days of the resolution; DIR-12 within 30 days of a director change; INC-22 within 30 days of a change of registered office; BEN-2 within 30 days of a significant-beneficial-owner declaration; LLP Form 3 / Form 4 within 30 days of a change in the agreement or partners; and, for partnership firms, notice of a change in the firm, its partners or its place of business to the Registrar of Firms under the Indian Partnership Act, 1932. The Partnership Act itself has no annual return — a firm’s fixed dates come from income tax and GST.
