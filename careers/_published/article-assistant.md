## About this position

**Ashish Jayalata & Associates, Chartered Accountants**, Sahibabad, Ghaziabad, is looking for a motivated
**Article Assistant** to join our team. The articleship is built to give practical exposure to the work a CA
actually does — statutory and tax audits, income tax, GST, accounting, ROC and corporate compliance, and client
assignments — rather than confining you to a single kind of filing.

This is a learning position with real client work attached to it. Everything you prepare is reviewed by a
Chartered Accountant before it goes out, so you learn what is correct and why, not just what to type.

## Who we are looking for

Someone who is motivated to learn the profession properly, is comfortable asking questions, and takes care
over the records they handle. If you have just graduated, have done an internship, or have a year or two of
experience and want broader exposure, we would like to speak with you.

If you are looking for a professional learning environment with hands-on exposure across different areas of CA
practice, we would be happy to hear from you.
