## About this position

We are looking for an Article Assistant to join our team in Sahibabad, Ghaziabad. This is a hands-on
compliance role: you will prepare returns, keep records straight and make sure nothing is missed on a due
date. Most of the client work is from businesses in and around Ghaziabad — Sahibabad, Vaishali,
Vasundhara, Indirapuram and Rajendra Nagar — so you will see how a compliance issue actually affects a
running business.

The role suits someone who is organised and methodical rather than someone chasing a headline
qualification. If you have just graduated, have done an internship, or have one or two years of experience
and want to learn the profession properly, we would like to speak with you.

## What the work looks like

Your day will be divided between preparing returns for clients, checking that the numbers behind those
returns are supported by records, and staying ahead of deadlines. You will report to CA Ashish Rajput
directly, and the work you prepare will go out under a Chartered Accountant's review.

This is a learning position. We expect you to ask questions, and we expect you to become someone who can
handle a client file independently within a year.

## Who we are looking for

Someone who is careful with documents, honest when a figure does not add up, and comfortable telling a
client that something needs attention before a deadline rather than after it. Accuracy matters more than
speed in this work.

If that is how you work, please apply with your CV.
