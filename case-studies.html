Income Tax

Advance Tax Mismatch & Return Correction for a Proprietor

A trading business proprietor in Sahibabad had not paid advance tax in the prescribed instalments and faced potential interest under Sections 234B and 234C of the Income Tax Act.

  • Recomputed income with year-end provisions and reviewed quarterly projections.
  • Prepared accurate estimates and reconciled taxes paid against the books.
  • Filed the return with correctly computed interest as per the Act.
Outcome: Returns filed accurately with interest correctly computed under law, and a clear tax position maintained for the client.
GST

Input Tax Credit Reconciliation for a Manufacturer

A manufacturing unit in Ghaziabad had discrepancies between GSTR-2B data and its purchase records, leading to blocked input tax credit.

  • Reconciled purchase registers with GSTR-2B table-by-table.
  • Identified missing vendor registrations and ineligible credit lines.
  • Filed amendments to GSTR-1 and rectified the monthly returns.
Outcome: Genuine credit restored where eligible, ineligible claims reversed, and the mismatch resolved before the annual return was filed.
Registrations

MSME (Udyam) Registration & Import Export Code for a Trader

A small trader in Indirapuram required MSME registration to avail government scheme benefits and an Import Export Code (IEC) to begin importing.

  • Prepared the business profile and assisted with Udyam registration.
  • Documented the IEC application and coordinated with the bank for digital signature support.
  • Advised on classification and basic compliance under the Foreign Trade Policy.
Outcome: Both registrations completed, and the client began importing within the same quarter.
Audit

Statutory Audit of a Private Limited Company

A private limited company in the NCR was due for its annual statutory audit under the Companies Act, 2013.

  • Audited the financial statements in accordance with applicable Standards on Auditing.
  • Reviewed internal controls, statutory dues, and related-party transactions.
  • Filed the audit report in the MCA portal within the prescribed timelines.
Outcome: Audit completed and financial statements filed on time with the Registrar of Companies.
