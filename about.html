About

Profile, qualifications, and practice credentials

Professional Profile

CA Ashish Rajput is a practicing Chartered Accountant registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), based in Sahibabad, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. The practice provides comprehensive financial, tax, and compliance services to individuals, proprietorships, partnerships, and small-to-medium enterprises across the NCR region.

Areas of Practice

The practice handles Income Tax compliance including ITR filing, tax planning, assessments, and appeals. GST services cover registration, monthly and quarterly return filing, annual audit, and litigation support. Audit and Assurance engagements include statutory audit, internal audit, tax audit, and compliance reviews for businesses of varying sizes.

Additional areas of practice include company and LLP incorporation, ROC compliance, annual filings, day-to-day accounting and bookkeeping, and business advisory services including virtual CFO support, budgeting, and business planning.

Practice Philosophy

The practice is built on the principle of providing accurate, timely, and practical financial guidance. Every engagement is handled with the professional standards and ethical obligations mandated by ICAI's Code of Ethics. The focus is on building long-term relationships based on trust, competence, and reliability.
