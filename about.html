Professional Profile

Ashish Jayalata & Associates is a Chartered Accountancy practice based in Shalimar Garden, Sahibabad, Ghaziabad, established in 2023. The practice provides accounting, audit, direct and indirect tax, and business compliance services to individuals, proprietorships, partnerships, and companies across Ghaziabad and the wider NCR region.

The practice is registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). Our work covers income tax filing and planning, GST registration and compliance, statutory and internal audit, company and LLP incorporation, and related advisory and compliance work — described in full under Services.

Areas of Practice

The practice handles Income Tax compliance including ITR filing, tax planning, assessments, and appeals. GST services cover registration, monthly and quarterly return filing, reconciliation, and advisory. Audit and Assurance engagements include statutory audit, tax audit under Section 44AB, internal audit, and bank audit assignments.

Additional areas of practice include company and LLP incorporation, ROC compliance, annual filings, day-to-day accounting and bookkeeping, business plan and financial projection preparation, asset inventory and valuation, charity and trust audits, and corporate representation before tax and regulatory authorities.

Practice Approach

The practice is built on the principle of providing accurate, timely, and practical financial guidance. Every engagement is handled with the professional standards and ethical obligations mandated by ICAI's Code of Ethics. The focus is on building long-term relationships based on competence and reliability.
