Working at Ashish Jayalata & Associates

We are a Chartered Accountancy practice registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, serving individuals, proprietors, partnerships and companies across Ghaziabad and the wider NCR region — Sahibabad, Vaishali, Vasundhara, Indirapuram and Rajendra Nagar.

What working here means in practice: you handle engagements yourself rather than passing them down a chain. Audit and tax assignments are discussed with the CA who will sign off on them. Deadlines are tracked in a diary, not discovered the evening before. Our clients are local businesses who need accurate work, delivered on time, and who keep coming back because they get it.

We hire for aptitude and attitude rather than pedigree. If you want to learn the profession properly, in a practice where the work you submit goes out under a Chartered Accountant's signature, we would like to hear from you.

What We Offer

Direct mentorship

Work alongside a practising Chartered Accountant and see the reasoning behind an audit opinion, a tax position, or a client conversation.

Variety of work

Statutory audit, tax audit, GST, income tax, incorporation and advisory for real local businesses — not repetitive filing for a single client.

Exposure to compliance

Income tax, GST, MCA, EPFO and ESI work handled end to end, so you learn how the pieces fit together rather than just filing one return.

Local practice, real clients

A Sahibabad and Ghaziabad client base that returns year after year, which means the work has consequences beyond a filing checklist.
