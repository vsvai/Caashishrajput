Article Assistant

Sahibabad, Ghaziabad · Full Time

About this position

We are looking for an Article Assistant to join our team in Sahibabad, Ghaziabad. This is a hands-on compliance role: you will prepare returns, keep records straight and make sure nothing is missed on a due date. Most of the client work is from businesses in and around Ghaziabad — Sahibabad, Vaishali, Vasundhara, Indirapuram and Rajendra Nagar — so you will see how a compliance issue actually affects a running business.

The role suits someone who is organised and methodical rather than someone chasing a headline qualification. If you have just graduated, have done an internship, or have one or two years of experience and want to learn the profession properly, we would like to speak with you.

What the work looks like

Your day will be divided between preparing returns for clients, checking that the numbers behind those returns are supported by records, and staying ahead of deadlines. You will report to CA Ashish Rajput directly, and the work you prepare will go out under a Chartered Accountant's review.

This is a learning position. We expect you to ask questions, and we expect you to become someone who can handle a client file independently within a year.

Who we are looking for

Someone who is careful with documents, honest when a figure does not add up, and comfortable telling a client that something needs attention before a deadline rather than after it. Accuracy matters more than speed in this work.

If that is how you work, please apply with your CV.

Key Responsibilities

  • Prepare and file GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B) and reconcile GST data with the books of accounts
  • Prepare TDS returns and issue Form 16A certificates
  • Maintain day-to-day bookkeeping entries and assist with bank and ledger reconciliations
  • Collect and organise client documents, challans, invoices and supporting records before due dates
  • Assist with income tax return preparation for individuals, proprietors and small businesses
  • Track client compliance deadlines on a shared calendar and give advance reminders
  • Draft routine correspondence and assist with client queries on routine compliance matters

Skills Required

  • Working knowledge of Tally or any other accounting software
  • Familiarity with GST returns, TDS provisions and basic bookkeeping
  • Good working knowledge of Microsoft Excel
  • Careful attention to detail and the ability to follow a checklist accurately
  • Clear written communication in English and Hindi

Qualifications

  • B.Com, B.Sc (Commerce) or equivalent, or a CA Intermediate / IPCC student
  • Familiarity with the Income Tax Act and GST law is an advantage but not mandatory
  • A minimum of 6 months of internship experience in a CA firm is preferred; freshers are encouraged to apply

What We Offer

  • Direct training under a practising Chartered Accountant, not delegated to a junior on a large list
  • Exposure to statutory audit, tax audit, GST, income tax and advisory work
  • Fixed Monday to Saturday working week with a five-and-a-half day schedule
  • Performance-linked increments reviewed twice a year
  • A supportive, small team where questions are expected rather than avoided
